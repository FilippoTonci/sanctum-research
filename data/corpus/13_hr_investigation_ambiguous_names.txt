1. Background
On [[DATE_TIME|14 April 2025]], [[PERSON|Faith Goodwin]] (Account Manager, Sales) raised a grievance alleging that her line manager, [[PERSON|Mark Price]], made repeated comments about her age and excluded her from the [[?ORGANIZATION|Summit]] account handover. 3. Findings
3.1 [[PERSON|Joy]] corroborated that at the team offsite in [[LOCATION|Lake Geneva]] [[PERSON|Mr Price]] said, in front of the team, that [[PERSON|Faith]] was "past her best" and should "leave the big deals to the young guns".
3.2 [[PERSON|Mr Price]] accepts making a comment but says it was a joke. He says [[PERSON|Ms Goodwin]] was removed from the [[?ORGANIZATION|Summit]] account because the client, [[ORGANIZATION|Summit Health Partners]], requested a change. No evidence of that request was produced. The client contact, [[PERSON|Grant Fisher]], has not responded to our email.
3.3 Price lists and margin data referenced in the grievance were not relevant to the allegations. Summer pricing promotions were excluded from review.

4. Conclusion
The allegation of age-related comments is upheld. The allegation regarding the account removal is upheld on the balance of probabilities.

5. Recommendations
Disciplinary hearing for [[PERSON|Mr Price]]; mediation offered to [[PERSON|Ms Goodwin]]; refresher training for the Sales leadership team, including [[PERSON|Sunny]].
